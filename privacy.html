Privacy Policy

Orfinity · Last updated 29 October 2026

Orfinity runs two things this policy covers: the Prop Challenge Reality Check web page, and Echo, an app that publishes Orfinity's own videos and live streams to Orfinity's own YouTube channel. Contact: orfinital@gmail.com.

1. Prop Challenge Reality Check (this website)

  • Your trade file stays on your device. When you drop a trade-history file on the page, it is read and analysed inside your browser. It is not uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • No accounts, no tracking. The page has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising cookies and no tracking pixels. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of a normal web request.
  • If you email us. If you choose to email us your file for a written report, we receive what you send (your email address, the file and your message). We use it only to prepare your report and reply to you. We do not sell it, share it, or use it to trade. We delete the file within 90 days of sending your report, or sooner if you ask.

2. Echo (YouTube publishing app)

Echo is an internal tool. It signs in only with Orfinity's own Google account and is used only to manage Orfinity's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public, and it does not access any other person's Google account.

  • What Google data it uses: YouTube access for Orfinity's channel (scopes youtube and youtube.force-ssl) to upload videos, set titles, descriptions and thumbnails, manage live broadcasts and read live-chat messages on Orfinity's own streams; and read-only YouTube Analytics (yt-analytics.readonly) to see view counts and watch time for the channel.
  • Live-chat messages on Orfinity's streams are read to show and respond to them on the stream. They are not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising.
  • Storage: the sign-in token is stored only on Orfinity's own computer. Channel statistics are kept for internal reporting.
  • Sharing: we do not sell, rent or transfer Google user data to anyone, and we do not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • Google API Services User Data Policy. Echo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Echo uses YouTube API Services; see also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

3. Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email orfinital@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

4. Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.

Orfinity provides education and information only. It is not investment advice, does not manage money and does not trade on anyone's behalf.
